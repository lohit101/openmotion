# Influencer LoRA research — 2026-10-24

An identity LoRA and a realism/style LoRA address different goals. A character adapter can reinforce the identity it was trained on; it is not a general image-quality upgrade. A style adapter can bias texture, tone and photographic presentation. Whether that helps this character-replacement pipeline, or instead changes the established lighting, geometry or identity, requires a matched video comparison. These are hypotheses, not measured improvements on Recast.

Our checkpoint is SCAIL-2 INT8 in ComfyUI. Generic Wan 2.1 14B adapters can target overlapping transformer modules, but loading some weights does not establish complete compatibility with SCAIL-2's additional conditioning. Flux, SDXL and Wan 1.3B/5B adapters do not belong in these slots. Wan 2.2 dual-expert recipes are not automatically transferable to this single-model graph. The official [SCAIL-2 implementation](https://github.com/zai-org/SCAIL-2#lora-integrations) documents LightX2V, DPO and its replacement-specific relighting adapter; it does not validate arbitrary influencer adapters.

The two downloadable candidates were checked using their original publisher API records: [Smartphone record](https://civitai.com/api/v1/models/1763826) and [Instagooner record](https://civitai.com/api/v1/models/1836311). Both list downloads and broad commercial-use permissions at the time checked; these records do not establish OSI-style open-source status, training-data provenance or uncensored behavior. No weights were installed. Raw records are saved under ignored `backups/lora-research-20261024/`.

Recommendation: keep the measured Natural recipe as the baseline and leave additional LoRA slots at None. If the identity is already stable, first test one mild, model-compatible photographic-style adapter against that baseline. A starting sweep of 0.2 / 0.35 / 0.5 is a proposed experiment, not a publisher recommendation or a proven setting. Keep source, prepared reference, prompt, seed, frame count, resolution, relighting and sampler fixed; do not copy unrelated Wan 2.2 strength values or stack several adapters at once.

Judge synchronized frames and motion throughout the clip: face likeness during turns, skin texture without oversharpening, exposure/color stability across the continuation boundary, source-relative scale/position, hair/mask edges, hands and clothing detail. Watch the clips at normal speed as well as reviewing frames. Improvement in a single thumbnail is insufficient. No credible numerical video-quality comparison for these two adapters on SCAIL-2 was found, and no measured quality scores are assigned here. Adding more weights also adds download/storage and potentially loading/patching costs; exact rendering overhead needs measurement.
