# Project workflow

- Image replacement has an independent draft and immutable per-job target/reference/mask/face inputs. Use the selected image stack; no video models or adapters are required. The target controls composition, pose and scale; identity references control appearance. Masks bind to their target and protect exact decoded source pixels outside the edit area. Face crops/head geometry checks are visual diagnostics, never a fabricated identity score or guaranteed identity lock. Image and video work share the existing serialized queue and receipt recovery. An image extracted from a known video may return as a prepared frame; a generic photo must return as a normal reference with automatic preparation. Downloaded images can finish locally without a GPU; never pay to regenerate a locally recoverable output.
